# Inventory & Demand Analysis

## Overview

Analyzed inventory and order data to identify product demand patterns, fast- and slow-moving products, profitability, order-status trends, and warehouse performance.

## Objectives

* Analyze product demand and order quantities
* Identify fast- and slow-moving products
* Evaluate category and warehouse performance
* Analyze monthly demand and order-status patterns
* Identify products requiring operational attention

## Tools & Technologies

* **SQL Server (SSMS)** – Data analysis and business queries
* **Python** – Exploratory Data Analysis
* **Pandas & NumPy** – Data manipulation
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn** – Data visualization

## Key Analysis

* Product-level demand analysis
* Fast/slow-moving product identification
* Category performance analysis
* Warehouse performance analysis
* Monthly demand trends
* Order cancellation analysis
* Product profitability analysis

## Key Insights

* Identified high-demand products requiring closer inventory monitoring
* Highlighted slow-moving products with relatively lower demand
* Compared warehouse and category-level performance
* Analyzed cancellation patterns to identify potential operational issues

## Recommendations

* Prioritize operational monitoring for consistently high-demand products
* Review slow-moving products to reduce excess inventory risk
* Investigate products with high cancellation activity
* Use demand and warehouse-level trends to support inventory planning

## Dataset

The dataset contains order-level information including products, categories, warehouses, quantities, prices, profits, order status, and order dates.
